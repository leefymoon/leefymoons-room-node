the poverty 360

no rgh? *cries in a corner*

oh my sweet boy. this xbox is an xbox 360 E. which was the 360 released right before the xbox one... it also has a winchester mobo, meaning it cannot be hardmodded. how sad. oh but how i LOVED this thing... that is until it started overheating anytime i tried using it. so it sat unused for YEARS until 2025 when i decided to take it apart with my father. we changed the thermal paste and thought all would be fine and dandy. but! of course not. it still overheat so i gave up, yet again.

that leads us into january 2026. i was chatting with my friends as one does and somehow the xbox 360 came up. i remembered that my xbox 360 was just sitting collecting dust. i decided to just bite the bullet and open it up again! what do i discover? nothing. it seemed fine! new thermal paste and everything! that was until i turned it one without its case. its fan wasn't working! after all these years i had thought the fan was completely out of the question, but nope! so i ordered a new fan and all worked just fine! xbox one s

the xbox one s(hit edition)

this console was with me since 2017. this thing got me through all of the hard times up until it was the hard time. i got it for xmas in 2017 and i LOVED it. i played minecraft and roblox nearly everyday with my friend. as i grew old i played less and less, but never abandoned gaming completely.

fast forward to june-ish 2025, the xbox starts acting funny. immediately i knew, this thing is finally on its death bed. now by this point i was basically over console gaming and i had started gaming on my father's pc. like i predicted, the xbox started slowly bootting up more and more, until the point to when it would boot up it wouldn't even load the screen for my account pin. it even managed to corrupt some part of the xbox and i had to factory reset it! this is when i knew i had to let it go. so i said my goodbyes and swore to never use it again. then shortly after my father gave me his pc, so thank you for your sacrifice xbox.
